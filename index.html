About

Six years on the same problem, from a few different seats.

I work at Xorosoft, a cloud ERP platform built for retail, ecommerce, and wholesale businesses. My job is the unglamorous middle of commerce: making sure an order placed on a storefront shows up correctly in the warehouse, the invoice, and the books — without someone reconciling it by hand at month-end.

I've done this from the support desk, from customer success, and now as the product owner writing the requirements. That path means I rarely design for a workflow I haven't personally had to untangle for a client on a call.

Selected Work

Three problems in the post-order stack.

ENTRY 01 — PLATFORM CAPABILITY

A first-time B2B sync layer for Shopify

Live

Our Shopify integration only ever handled B2C order flow. B2B purchasing — company accounts, payment-terms-driven invoicing, credit memos — had no path into the ERP, so returns and accounting kept breaking wherever it came up.

Validation

Triangulated support tickets, direct client interviews, and sales conversations to confirm this was a recurring, systemic gap across our client base — not an isolated ask.

My Role

Built the business case for engineering to treat it as a core capability, owned requirements end to end, and worked with engineering leadership and sales through delivery.

Outcome

Shipped as a configurable platform feature. Adoption picked up quickly across our Shopify Plus client base within the first quarter post-launch.

ENTRY 02 — WORKFLOW AUTOMATION

Returns and exchange sync, end to end

Shipped

Refunds, credits, and exchanges were being reconciled by hand across three disconnected systems — the storefront, the ERP, and our shipping/returns partner — creating real risk of double credits, missed refunds, and stock counts that didn't match reality.

Scope

Automated RMA creation, return-shipment tracking, and refund, credit, and exchange handling — including equal, greater, and lesser-value exchanges — with real-time status sync back to every system.

My Role

Owned requirements and workflow design, defined the test plan across every return type, and coordinated delivery with engineering and QA.

Outcome

Manual reconciliation dropped for clients running the workflow, and support tickets tied to return mismatches fell measurably.

ENTRY 03 — CORE OWNERSHIP

Order-to-cash, day to day

Ongoing

Underneath the individual projects is the steady work: billing, invoicing, accounts receivable, collections, cash application, and revenue recognition, run across a large enterprise client base with monthly close on the line.

Cadence

Write PRDs and run sprint cycles with engineering, prioritizing fixes and features using SQL and usage data rather than the loudest request.

Stakeholders

Primary point of contact for enterprise CXOs, translating operational pain into requirements engineering can actually build against.

Outcome

Support satisfaction improved to 80% CSAT alongside a meaningful drop in repeat tickets, through fixing root causes rather than patching symptoms.
